# Code of conduct

We want these projects to be a place where anyone can ask, learn and contribute, whatever their experience, language, background or identity.

**Expected:** be respectful and patient, give and accept feedback kindly, assume good intent, and focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

**Not accepted:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discrimination, publishing someone's private information, and any conduct that would be out of place in a professional setting.

**Scope:** this applies in the issues, pull requests, discussions and every other space of these repositories.

**Reporting:** write to **info@totaljs.es**. Reports are treated privately. The maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock threads, or block people who do not follow this code.
